# 1\. Problem Statement

The Nepalese-Australian diaspora — numbering over 100,000 people across Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, and Adelaide — currently lacks a dedicated, reliable digital platform for news, cultural content, and community information tailored specifically to their unique context living in Australia.

## 1.1 The Core Problem

Nepalese-Australians must rely on either general Australian mainstream media (which does not cover diaspora-specific issues) or informal social media groups (which lack editorial standards and reliability). There is no professional, purpose-built news platform addressing:

*   News and events specific to the Nepalese community in Australia
*   Cultural content connecting the diaspora with Nepali heritage
*   Practical information relevant to immigrants — visa changes, remittances, community events
*   A platform that bridges both Nepali and English language audiences

## 1.2 Who Experiences This Problem

Primary users include Nepalese immigrants living in Australia, Nepali students enrolled in Australian universities, community organisations and cultural groups, and Australian citizens with Nepalese heritage seeking cultural connection.

## 1.3 Why It Matters in Australia

Australia is home to one of the fastest-growing South Asian diaspora communities. Nepalese migration to Australia has increased significantly over the past decade due to student visas and skilled migration pathways. The community contributes significantly to Australian society yet remains underserved by existing media. A dedicated platform fosters social cohesion, cultural preservation, and informed community participation.

## 1.4 Project Vision

Nepal News Australia will be a professional, full-stack web application providing verified news, community events, cultural content, and practical information for Nepalese-Australians. It will support multiple user roles for editorial control, multilingual content, and real-time community features.

## 1.5 Success Criteria

*   A fully functional news portal with articles across 8 categories
*   Role-based content management with Admin, Editor, Contributor, and Reader roles
*   Mobile-responsive design accessible across all devices
*   Search, categorisation, and social sharing capabilities
*   Newsletter subscription and community event management
*   Live API integrations for weather, currency, and culturally relevant content
