# 1\. Executive Summary

This report analyses the competitive landscape for Nepali-Australian digital media. The research identifies five key competitors, analyses their features, and defines the gap that Nepal News Australia fills.

# 2\. Competitor Analysis

## 2.1 Top 5 Competitors

| Platform | Audience | Strengths | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SBS Nepali | Broad diaspora | Government funded, multilingual | No community CMS |
| Setopati | Nepal-based readers | Professional journalism | Not Australia-focused |
| The Himalayan Times | Nepal domestic | Strong editorial team | No diaspora content |
| Nepali Facebook Groups | Nepalese-Australians | Large user base | No editorial standards |
| Indian Link | Indian-Australians | Strong CMS, events | Not Nepali-specific |

## 2.2 Market Gap

No existing platform combines: (1) Australia-specific Nepali content, (2) professional CMS with editorial roles, (3) bilingual English/Nepali support, and (4) community features like events and newsletters.

# 3\. Target Market

*   Primary: Nepalese immigrants and students in Australia (~100,000+)
*   Secondary: Community organisations, event organisers, cultural groups
*   Tertiary: Non-Nepalese Australians interested in Nepali culture

# 4\. Market Opportunity

Nepal is Australia's 8th largest source of international students. The Nepalese community is concentrated in Sydney (40%), Melbourne (30%), Brisbane (15%), and other cities. The market is underserved, engaged, and growing — making it an ideal target for a dedicated digital media platform.

# 5\. Conclusion

Nepal News Australia is positioned to be the first professionally managed, full-stack news platform dedicated to the Nepalese-Australian diaspora. The gap in the market is clear, the audience is defined, and the community need is urgent.
